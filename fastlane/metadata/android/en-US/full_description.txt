OnionRoute Lite is a minimal, lightweight Tor client for Android that provides VPN mode, SOCKS5/HTTP proxy, and censorship circumvention through Snowflake and obfs4 bridges.

<b>Features:</b>
• One-tap Tor connect/disconnect
• VPN mode: route all device traffic through Tor
• SOCKS5 proxy on 127.0.0.1:9050 for apps that support proxy settings
• HTTP CONNECT proxy on 127.0.0.1:8118
• Snowflake bridge for censorship circumvention (WebRTC-based)
• obfs4 bridge support with built-in default bridges
• .onion site access (automatic via Tor routing)
• Real-time bandwidth monitoring (speed + cumulative totals for upload and download)
• Kill switch: block all internet if Tor disconnects unexpectedly
• Auto-start on boot (optional)
• Foreground service with persistent notification

<b>Designed for low-end Android devices</b> with minimal RAM and storage. Optimized for Android 6.0 (Marshmallow) and above.

<b>This app does NOT include:</b>
• Built-in browser (use Firefox with your Tor connection)
• Root mode or iptables manipulation
• Hidden service hosting
• Proprietary analytics, tracking, or Google Play Services

All native binaries (Tor, obfs4proxy, Snowflake, hev-socks5-tunnel) are built from source during the F-Droid build process. No prebuilt proprietary binaries are included.
